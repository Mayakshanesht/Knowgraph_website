Knowgraph — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026. Knowgraph ("we") is operated from Germany and this policy follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What we store

  • Account: when you sign in, your authentication identity (email address and a user id) is managed by Supabase; we link your learning data to a pseudonymous id derived from it.
  • Learning progress: which reels you watched, quiz answers, mastery states and streaks — so your progress follows your account across devices.
  • Subscription status: your plan tier, its expiry, and references from our payment providers. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Content you create: courses you generate or upload, and text/PDFs you submit for reel generation.

What we send to processors

  • Cloudflare (EU/global edge): hosts our API, database and media storage.
  • Supabase: authentication and course enrollment records.
  • Groq: the text you submit in the Learn tab is processed by their language-model API to author reels.
  • Payment providers (Stripe/Razorpay): process purchases on our website; we receive only confirmation events.

Playground

Images or videos you run through the Playground are processed transiently on our GPU server for the single inference and are not retained.

What we do not do

No advertising, no sale of personal data, no third-party analytics SDKs, no tracking across other apps.

Legal basis & retention

We process data to provide the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and with your consent where asked in-app (Art. 6(1)(a)). Data is kept while your account exists.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export, or erase your data (GDPR Arts. 15–20). Deleting your account inside the app (Profile → Delete account) permanently removes your progress, requests, enrollments and generated content. For anything else, contact mayurwaghchoure1995@gmail.com. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
